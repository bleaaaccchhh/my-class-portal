What

Universal design is designing for the widest range of users without special adaptation.

Where it comes from

Architect Ronald Mace and a team at NC State coined the term and wrote the principles in 1997.

The seven principles

  1. Equitable use
  2. Flexibility in use
  3. Simple and intuitive use
  4. Perceptible information
  5. Tolerance for error
  6. Low physical effort
  7. Size and space for approach and use

WCAG: perceivable, operable, understandable, robust

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines turn these ideas into testable rules. Sites aim for level AA.

A spectrum, not a category

Permanent, temporary, and situational limits all shape how people use the web.
