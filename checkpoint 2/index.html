About Me

Hi, I'm Finn Stevens, a senior at CU Boulder studying marketing with a minor in Creative Technology & Design. I'm a member of the Leeds Scholars Program and the CU Music Industry Club. My goal is to build a career in the music industry, where I can combine creativity, business strategy, and data to support artists, live events, and music experiences. I'm taking this course because it is required for my minor, but I'm excited to be here.

Outside of class, I make music, design graphics, and edit photos. I also love listening to music, hanging out with friends, and being present in everyday life. This semester I'm learning how to build websites that work for everyone, from semantic HTML to screen readers. This portal collects each of my coding projects in one place, so please explore.
