Why room size matters

Small rooms, big rooms, and the open air

The same artist can feel completely different depending on the venue. A club of a few hundred people puts the crowd close to the stage and often costs less to attend. A ballroom of several thousand adds production, lights, and a bigger sound system. An outdoor amphitheater adds scenery and weather.

Colorado’s Front Range is unusual because it packs all three into a short drive. Boulder’s historic theaters sit about 30 miles from Denver’s ballrooms, and Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison is carved into the foothills between them.
